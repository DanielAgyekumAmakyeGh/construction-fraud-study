#  Occupational Fraud & COSO Internal Controls in Ghana's Industrial Construction Subsector

Welcome! Thank you for visiting the official repository and research portal for this academic study. 

This repository hosts the interactive digital survey application developed for Master's thesis research at **University Ghana**. The study investigates occupational fraud dynamics, collusive mechanisms, and the operational effectiveness of the **COSO (2013) Internal Control Framework** across industrial construction projects in Ghana (including mining facilities, energy installations, and heavy infrastructure).

---

##  An Invitation to Industry Experts

As a qualified professional operating within Ghana’s industrial construction subsector (such as a Project Manager, Quantity Surveyor, Internal Auditor, or Finance Officer), **your practical insights are invaluable**. 

Construction projects face unique governance vulnerabilities due to remote site operations, complex multi-tier subcontracting, and high-capital material flows. This research aims to bridge the gap between theoretical internal controls and ground-truth operational realities.

### Why Your Participation Matters
* **Empirical Impact:** Help generate data-driven insights that identify structural control breakdowns and improve risk mitigation frameworks across the industry.
* **Strict Confidentiality:** All responses are completely anonymized and aggregated. No individual, project, or corporate names will be disclosed in academic publications or public reports.
* **Time Efficiency:** The interactive survey takes approximately **10 to 15 minutes** to complete.
